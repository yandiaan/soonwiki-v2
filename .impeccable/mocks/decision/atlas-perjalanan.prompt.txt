Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity mobile-first web homepage concept for SoonWiki
Primary request: Design a complete polished portrait 1024x1536 mobile homepage UI for SoonWiki, a public Indonesian community directory and collective archive. Direction name: "Atlas Perjalanan". Structure from top to bottom: compact navigation with SoonWiki wordmark and menu icon; hero headline "Di mana Soonies sekarang?"; supporting sentence "Temukan perjalanan, karya, dan hal-hal yang mereka banggakan."; a prominent search control with exact placeholder "Cari nama, perusahaan, atau keahlian"; a route-map-like discovery field containing several human portrait profile stops with concise labels such as "Muhammad Faisal · Video Producer · Tempo" and "Soon 2018"; a restrained community numbers strip with labels "Soonies", "Perusahaan", "Pencapaian"; then the beginning of a section titled "Baru dari komunitas".
Style/medium: premium production-grade mobile UI mockup, modern editorial interface, humane rather than corporate
Composition/framing: portrait phone viewport shown straight-on without a device frame; dominant search and route map in first viewport; large readable Indonesian typography; clear touch targets; enough second-fold content to prove the system
Color palette: warm bone paper, deep forest ink, vivid vermilion route accent, restrained cobalt utility accent
Materials/textures: subtle enamel-signage lines, fine paper grain, ticket-notch details used sparingly, real-feeling editorial portrait photography
Motion implication: route line prepared to draw on scroll, profile stops stagger into place, count digits can flip; keep the still screen calm
Text (verbatim): "SoonWiki", "Di mana Soonies sekarang?", "Temukan perjalanan, karya, dan hal-hal yang mereka banggakan.", "Cari nama, perusahaan, atau keahlian", "Muhammad Faisal", "Video Producer", "Tempo", "Soon 2018", "Soonies", "Perusahaan", "Pencapaian", "Baru dari komunitas", "From Soon, Everywhere."
Constraints: Bahasa Indonesia is primary; people and portraits are visually central; warm, community-driven, editorial, slightly nostalgic; accessible contrast; no invented logos; no fake testimonials; no dashboard chrome; no excessive cards; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no watermark
